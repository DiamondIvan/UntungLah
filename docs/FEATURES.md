# Features

## My Business

Create a profile or load the fictional Warung Nasi Lemak. Record sales with a quantity and selling price, purchases with a cash amount and operating expenses such as a stall fee. View the entries and delete mistakes. Saved entries drive the financial snapshot.

| Dashboard measure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Recorded revenue | Total of recorded product sales |
| Recorded cash outflows | Total of recorded purchases and operating expenses |
| Recorded cash surplus | Recorded inflows minus recorded outflows |
| Unit contribution | Selling price minus estimated variable cost for one unit |

## Learning Path

Study Record Keeping, Cost Calculation, Pricing Strategy, Profit Management, Cash Flow and Business Growth. Each topic includes a learning objective, a lesson, a Malaysian example and a short multiple-choice quiz. The quiz shows feedback and an explanation and supports retrying. Quiz attempts persist in the current browser.

## Practice Lab

**Pricing Decisions** compares price, estimated unit cost and a user-set sales quantity. **Ingredient Cost Increase** explores how absorbing a cost increase, changing price or reducing variable cost affects contribution under an explicit quantity assumption.

Each scenario shows revenue, estimated variable cost, gross contribution, contribution per unit and gross margin. Gross margin divides unit contribution by selling price. Markup divides it by estimated variable cost. A zero denominator leaves the ratio unavailable. Break-even units divide fixed costs by positive unit contribution and round up.

## AI Coach

Ask a question or request an explanation of a scenario. The live integration uses a business summary and calculated comparison where applicable. The response offers relevant lessons or actions with assumptions and limitations. With no provider connection, the interface labels the built-in demo explanation.

## My Progress

Create an improvement goal with a title, financial topic and optional target date. Save a suggested action from the coaching flow or create one directly. Completed actions and quiz activity are available when you return to the workspace.
